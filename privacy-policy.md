# To the Moon — Privacy Policy



Last updated: 8 October 2026
App: To the Moon (com.tothemoon.twin_thumb)
Publisher: The independent developer of To the Moon
Contact: developer.gagan985@gmail.com

## About this policy
This policy explains how information is handled when you use To the Moon, an Android rocket reflex game.

## Game data on your device
You do not need to create an account to play. The game stores your sound preference and the time an advertisement was last shown on your device, so settings are remembered and advertisements can be spaced apart. The game code does not upload your score or gameplay progress to a developer-operated server. Consent choices may also be stored by Google's consent tools.

## Advertising and third-party services
The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show occasional advertisements when you choose to retry a flight. The SDK can collect and share IP addresses, device and advertising identifiers, app and advertisement interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. An IP address may be used to estimate approximate location. This information is used to deliver and measure advertisements, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. Processing depends on your choices, device settings, and the advertisement served.

Google processes information under its own privacy policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information about Google's advertising technologies:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

The game does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

## Your choices
Where applicable, Google's consent tools present advertising privacy choices. When available, you can reopen them using Settings > Ad privacy options in the game. You can also use the advertising privacy controls in Android settings. Changing advertising choices does not necessarily stop all advertisements or all data processing needed to deliver them.

## Retention and deletion
Local preferences remain until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Android backup and restore settings may preserve or restore app data. Data already received by Google is subject to Google's retention and deletion practices; clearing the game's storage does not delete that information from Google's systems. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention for details.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled by the developer, contact the email above. You do not need an account in the game to make a request.

## Contact information you provide
If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use this information to answer your request and resolve support or privacy issues. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us.

## Security
Google states that information transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No storage or transmission method can provide an absolute security guarantee.

## Children
To the Moon is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact developer.gagan985@gmail.com so we can investigate and delete information we hold where appropriate. Advertising data processed by Google is handled as described above.

## Changes and contact
This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will appear on this page with a revised date. Contact developer.gagan985@gmail.com with questions.
